GrapeMinds in the WineBro Console

Operating guide for admins · 10 October 2026 · Console: https://winebro-console.web.app

What this is. GrapeMinds is a wine database (311,480 wines). We have a free key: 250 calls per month. The console lets you spend those calls carefully, keeps every answer, and helps you attach the data to our drinks so customers see it in the WineBro app.

The rule in one line: nothing is called until you confirm the price, nothing is paid twice, and nothing reaches customers until you map it and publish it.

1. Before you start

  • Sign in to the console with your Google account (ravi.p.sankar@gmail.com or dushyantb11@gmail.com).
  • GrapeMinds covers wines only. Whisky, rum, gin, brandy, vodka and beer are not in it.
  • Calls reset on the 1st of every month. The console stops at a ceiling of 230 to keep a reserve (you can change this under Terms & budget).

2. Where to find it

Sidebar→Data Cellar→GrapeMinds card→GrapeMinds dashboard

Data Cellar shows one card per outside source (GrapeMinds, Open Food Facts, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, X-Wines, BJCP, Indian Food 101, Spirits Database). Each card tells you what the source provides, how many of our records use it and its licence. Only connected sources open a dashboard; today that is GrapeMinds.

4. Downloading data (spending calls)

Every action follows the same three steps:

1 Press "price it"→2 Read: "This will use N calls · X left → Y afterwards"→3 Press "Use N calls"

If there are not enough calls left, the button is disabled and nothing happens.

About the text fields. GrapeMinds generates descriptions, tasting notes and food pairings on demand (machine-written). The console stores them under "generated" and the app will never show them as facts. Grapes, producer, region, sugar level and LWIN are catalogue facts.

6. Mapping GrapeMinds data to our drinks

Open the dashboard → Map to our drinks. Each row is one of our wines with up to five GrapeMinds candidates, scored by how well the names match.

  1. If a candidate shows a ↓, its details are not downloaded yet. Clicking it prices a 1-call detail download; confirm it.
  2. Decide whether to switch on "Also adopt acidity, tannin and body from GrapeMinds". On: those three scores replace our estimates and the label changes to say so. Off: the profile is stored under open data only.
  3. Click the right candidate. A sheet shows what will be written: GrapeMinds id/LWIN, grapes, flavour profile, licence state.
  4. Press Save as draft. The console writes a draft of the drink: openData.grapeminds plus empty fields filled (grape variety, region, style). The live drink is untouched.

Adding a wine we do not have

Downloaded wines tab → row with details → Add as drink. A draft drink is created with category (from colour/type), name, producer, region, grapes, and acidity/tannin/body from the profile; fruit, freshness and complexity start at 5 and stay "estimated". You then edit it like any drink.

No candidate for a wine? Use Download → Search by name with the producer and wine name, then come back to the Map tab.

7. Making it visible to customers

Draft saved→Drink page: review→Preview in WineBro→Publish now / Releases → Publish→Live in the app
  1. Open the drink (Drinks → the row shows a draft chip, or click the name in the Map tab).
  2. Check the filled fields and the Open data → grapeminds block. Adjust anything you like.
  3. Press Preview in WineBro. The real app opens in a phone frame with your draft. Check the product sheet (grape, region, open facts) and, for a dish, the ranking.
  4. Publish: Publish now on the drink page, or collect several drafts and publish them together under Releases (one release id, one-click roll back).
  5. The app picks it up on the next screen open. No app update is needed.

8. Licence rule — read before publishing

Publishing a drink that carries GrapeMinds data is blocked until one of these is true:

  1. The wine is licensed (GrapeMinds sells a per-wine licence, about €0.38, billed on paid plans). Future console button; today ask engineering to run it for the chosen wines.
  2. GrapeMinds has confirmed in writing that our plan allows storing and showing their data. Record it under Terms & budget (tick the box, paste the confirmation). The block then lifts for all mapped wines.

Downloading, mapping, drafting and previewing are always allowed — they do not publish anything.

10. Troubleshooting

You seeWhat it means / what to do
"Not enough calls left this month"The ceiling is reached. Wait for the 1st, or raise the ceiling under Terms & budget (max 250).
"GrapeMinds answered 401/403"The key is wrong or expired. Engineering resets the secret GRAPEMINDS_API_KEY.
"GrapeMinds answered 429"Too fast (5 per second) or their monthly limit. Wait a minute and retry; the call was still counted by them.
A candidate shows ↓Details not downloaded yet; click it and confirm 1 call.
"GrapeMinds data is not licensed for publication yet"See section 8.
Nothing changes in the app after publishingOpen another screen or restart the app; the catalogue refreshes on the next screen open.
