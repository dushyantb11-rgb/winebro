1. Who can use WineBro

WineBro is for people who are of legal drinking age in their state or country. In India this is 21 or 25 depending on the state. By creating an account you confirm that you meet the legal drinking age where you live. We may close an account that does not.

2. What WineBro is

WineBro is a food-and-drink pairing companion. It suggests drinks for meals and meals for drinks, lets you scan a label, keep a tasting journal and track what you liked. WineBro does not sell alcohol, take orders or deliver. Any shop links, when enabled, lead to third-party sellers whose terms apply.

3. Advice, estimates and sources

  • Pairing suggestions are opinions generated by our rules and your taste profile. They are not professional, medical or dietary advice.
  • Taste scores marked "Estimated from published facts" are estimates with a stated confidence; drinks marked verified have been checked by a person.
  • Product facts and photos may come from open data sources credited inside the app (Settings → Photo credits). We correct mistakes when they are reported.

4. Your account and your content

  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Your journal entries, photos and voice notes remain yours. You give us permission to store and show them to you, and, only where you choose, to share aggregated, non-identifying signals (for example "12 people paired this with biryani this week").
  • You can delete your account from Settings. This removes your profile, journal, photos, voice notes, wishlist, friends, device tokens and contact-matching data.

5. Drink responsibly

WineBro encourages moderate, lawful enjoyment. Do not use the app to drink and drive, to supply alcohol to anyone under the legal age, or in any way that breaks local law. Rewards in the app (XP, badges) recognise tasting notes and learning, never the amount you drink.

6. Fair use

Do not copy the catalogue, scrape the service, interfere with other users, or try to tamper with rankings, scores or community signals. We may suspend accounts that do.
